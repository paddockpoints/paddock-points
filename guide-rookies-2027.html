Rookies to watch: who could reach F1 next

Which junior drivers have the super licence points and the backing to step up? A look at the 2026 F2 and F3 front-runners, including the F1 practice running that tops up their totals.

Updated 17 November 2026, after the São Paulo GP. F2 has two rounds left, at Lusail and the Abu Dhabi finale. Check the Road to F1 tracker for the latest standings.

Already in F1

Rafael Câmara made his F1 debut for Haas at the 2026 São Paulo Grand Prix, standing in for Esteban Ocon for the rest of the season. Haas had already signed him for 2027 alongside Oliver Bearman. He leads the F2 championship, and a top-three finish there is worth the full 40 super licence points on its own.

F2 contenders

Nikola Tsolov has been Câmara's closest rival all season. He banked 25 points as 2025 F3 runner-up and added 1 more for an F1 practice run with Racing Bulls in Mexico, so he is well past 40 whatever happens at the finale.

Alex Dunne sits third in F2, which would make him eligible on this season's result alone. He also has 2 points from F1 practice running with McLaren in 2025.

Gabriele Minì is fourth, worth 30 points as things stand, plus 1 from an F1 practice session with Alpine in Brazil. A podium in the championship would take him to 40.

Dino Beganovic will be Ferrari's F1 reserve and development driver in 2027. Fifth in F2 is worth 20 points, and he has 4 more from F1 practice sessions with Ferrari, with another due at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

Why F1 practice matters

Every FP1 session a junior driver runs in an F1 car earns 1 super licence point, as long as they complete at least 100 km, up to 10 points in total. It sounds small, but for a driver finishing just outside the F2 top three, a handful of practice runs can close the gap to 40. Not every outing counts: in Brazil, Beganovic managed only 11 laps, well short of the distance needed. Read more in what a driver needs for an F1 super licence.

F3 stars heading to F2

Ugo Ugochukwu won the 2026 F3 title at the Madring finale, becoming the first American F3 champion. The title is worth 30 super licence points, so a solid F2 season would take him past 40.

Freddie Slater finished runner-up, worth 25 points. He has also driven for Audi in F1 practice, though neither session was long enough to earn a point. Tuukka Taponen, third, is expected to move up to F2 for 2027.

What decides it

Points are only the entry ticket. Seats depend on academy backing, team needs and timing. A driver can reach 40 points and still wait years for a seat, or step up quickly because a team has a vacancy at the right moment, as Câmara did.
