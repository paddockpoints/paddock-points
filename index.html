Penalty points Road to F1

A new F1 driver needs 40 super licence eligibility points, earned by championship finishing position across the current and previous two seasons. This tracks the 2026 support-series drivers and what their position is worth.

Eligibility points by finishing position. F2: 40, 40, 40, 30, 20, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3. F3: 30, 25, 20, 15, 12, 9, 7, 5, 3, 2. Banked points from earlier seasons are shown only where confirmed, so some drivers will hold more than shown here. Up to 10 extra points can come from F1 practice running. Sources: 2026 points table, F2 standings, F3 standings, 2027 moves.
