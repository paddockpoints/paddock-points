Penalty points Road to F1

A new F1 driver needs 40 super licence eligibility points, earned by championship finishing position across the current and previous two seasons. This tracks the 2026 support-series drivers and what their position is worth.

Eligibility points by finishing position. F2: 40, 40, 40, 30, 20, 10, 8, 6, 4, 3. F3: 30, 25, 20, 15, 12, 9, 7, 5, 3, 2. Banked points from earlier seasons are shown only where confirmed, so some drivers will hold more than shown here. F1 practice adds 1 point per FP1 session of 100 km or more in 2025 and 2026, up to 10, and is counted in each total. Grey cells on each bar are points from earlier seasons and F1 practice. Data from official F2 and F3 championship results and F1 practice classifications.

FIA penalty points on every 2026 F1 driver's licence. Points drop off 12 months after they were issued; 12 live points means a one-race ban.

Dates are when each point expires. Pick a date in the next 12 months to see each driver's balance once older points fall away. Projections only include points already given, so new penalties can change them. Data from FIA stewards' decisions.
